# Change the app language

The app is available in English and Arabic.

1. Open **Profile and settings**.
2. Tap **Language**.
3. Choose **English** or **العربية**.

The app switches immediately, including right-to-left layout for Arabic. The AppCompass assistant answers in the language you choose; you can also simply ask your question in Arabic or English.
