# How your data is used

Your bookings and profile are stored by the clinic and used only to provide your care and run the app.

The AppCompass assistant answers questions about using the app. It does not see your appointments, medical records or profile, and it does not need your personal details; please do not type them into the chat. Questions are logged without your identity so the clinic can improve the help articles.

To download or delete your data, open **Help**, then **Privacy**, then **My data**.
