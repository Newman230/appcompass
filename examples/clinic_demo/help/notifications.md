# Manage notifications and reminders

By default the app sends a reminder 24 hours and 2 hours before each appointment.

1. Open **Profile and settings**.
2. Tap **Notifications**.
3. Turn reminders, booking confirmations and clinic news on or off.

If you get no notifications at all, check that notifications are allowed for the app in your phone's settings. Booking confirmations are also sent by SMS and cannot be turned off.
