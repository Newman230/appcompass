# Running late

Please arrive 15 minutes before an in-person appointment to check in.

If you will be more than 10 minutes late, the clinic may need to move you to a later slot or ask you to reschedule. The app cannot hold your slot automatically; call the clinic branch from the appointment details screen (tap the appointment, then **Call branch**).

For video visits, the waiting room stays open until 10 minutes after the start time.
