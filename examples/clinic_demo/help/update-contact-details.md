# Update your phone number or email

1. Open **Profile and settings**.
2. Tap **Contact details**.
3. Edit your mobile number or email address and tap **Save**.

A verification code is sent to the new number or email. Enter it to finish the change. Until you verify, notifications keep going to your old details.

Your name and date of birth cannot be changed in the app; contact support with your ID to correct them.
