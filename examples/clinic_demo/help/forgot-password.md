# Can't sign in or forgot password

On the sign-in screen, tap **Forgot password**, enter your registered mobile number, and follow the code sent by SMS to set a new password.

If you changed your number and no longer have the old one, you cannot reset the password in the app. Contact support from the **Help** screen; you will need to confirm your identity.

After five wrong attempts, sign-in is locked for 15 minutes.
