# Contact clinic support

Open the **Help** screen and tap **Contact support** to send a message, or call the support line shown there.

Support hours are Sunday to Thursday, 7:00 to 21:00, and Saturday, 8:00 to 14:00. Messages sent outside these hours are answered the next working day.

Support can help with the app, bookings and your account. For medical questions, book an appointment. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
