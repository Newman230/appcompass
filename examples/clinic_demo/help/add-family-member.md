# Add a family member

You can book for children and other dependents from your own account.

1. Open **Profile and settings**.
2. Tap **Family members**, then **Add family member**.
3. Enter their full name, date of birth and relationship to you, and add their ID number.
4. Tap **Save**.

Children under 18 can be added directly. Adults need to approve the link from their own account. Once added, choose them under **Who is this visit for?** when booking.
