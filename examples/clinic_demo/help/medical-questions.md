# Medical questions and emergencies

The app and the assistant cannot give medical advice, interpret symptoms or test results, or recommend medicines.

For a medical question, book an appointment with a doctor, or choose a **Video visit** for a faster consultation.

In an emergency, call your local emergency number or go to the nearest emergency department. Do not use the app or the assistant for emergencies.
