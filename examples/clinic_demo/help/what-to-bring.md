# What to bring to your appointment

For in-person visits, bring:

- Your national ID or passport
- Your insurance card, if you have one
- A list of any medicines you currently take
- Any previous reports the doctor should see

The appointment details screen shows the branch address, a map link and parking information. Tap **Add to calendar** to save the visit with its address to your phone calendar.
