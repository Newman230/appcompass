# Reschedule an appointment

You can move an upcoming appointment to another time without cancelling it.

1. Open **My appointments**.
2. Tap the upcoming appointment you want to move.
3. Tap **Reschedule**.
4. Pick a new date and time slot. You can keep the same doctor or tap **Change doctor**.
5. Tap **Confirm new time**.

Your old slot is released as soon as you confirm. You can reschedule up to 2 hours before the appointment starts. Closer than that, the Reschedule button is hidden; contact support instead.
