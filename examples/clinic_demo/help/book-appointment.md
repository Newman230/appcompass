# Book an appointment

You can book a visit in a few taps.

1. Tap **Book** on the Home screen, or open the **Book appointment** screen.
2. Choose a specialty, for example Family Medicine or Dermatology.
3. Choose a clinic branch, then a doctor. Tap **Any available doctor** to see the earliest slots.
4. Pick a date and time slot. Greyed-out slots are already taken.
5. Choose **In person** or **Video visit**.
6. Choose who the visit is for: you or a family member on your profile.
7. Tap **Confirm**. The appointment appears under **My appointments** and you get a confirmation notification.

You can hold up to three upcoming appointments at a time.
