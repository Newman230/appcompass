# Clinic opening hours

Most branches are open Sunday to Thursday, 7:00 to 22:00, and Saturday, 8:00 to 16:00. Branches are closed on Friday.

Each branch's exact hours, address and phone number are on the **Home** screen under **Our branches**, and on every appointment's details screen.

Hours can change on public holidays; the Home screen shows a banner when they do.
