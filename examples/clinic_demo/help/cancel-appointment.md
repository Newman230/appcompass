# Cancel an appointment

1. Open **My appointments**.
2. Tap the upcoming appointment.
3. Tap **Cancel appointment**, choose a reason (optional), and tap **Yes, cancel**.

Cancelling at least 2 hours before the start time is free. Late cancellations and missed appointments are recorded on your profile; after three in 90 days, online booking is paused for 30 days and you need to book by phone.

Cancelled appointments move to the **Past** tab with the label Cancelled.
