# Join a video visit

Video visits are booked like in-person visits: choose **Video visit** when booking.

To join:

1. Open **My appointments** up to 10 minutes before the start time.
2. Tap the appointment, then **Join video visit**.
3. Allow camera and microphone access when asked.

You wait in a virtual waiting room until the doctor admits you. Use a stable Wi-Fi or 4G connection. If the call drops, tap **Join video visit** again; the doctor will see you rejoin.
